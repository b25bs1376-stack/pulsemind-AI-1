PulseMind AI

Detecting silent health risks before they become illness

Problem

Many physical and mental health conditions begin with subtle signals such as fatigue, emotional numbness, sleep disturbance, or cognitive overload. These signals are often ignored until serious illness appears.

Solution

PulseMind AI is a preventive AI healthcare platform that continuously analyzes weak physical, mental, and emotional patterns to detect early health risks—before symptoms become visible.

Core Features

Silent Symptom Detector
Mental Load Index
Emotional Side-Effect Predictor
Chronic Day-Quality Tracker
Health Honesty Reflector
Health Translator

How It Works

Users complete a short daily check-in. PulseMind AI analyzes trends over time using ethical AI models and provides early risk insights and gentle guidance.

Technology Stack

Microsoft Azure OpenAI, Azure Cognitive Services, and Azure Machine Learning enable scalable, secure, and responsible AI analysis.

Ethics & Safety

PulseMind AI does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace medical professionals. It is designed purely for preventive awareness, transparency, and user control.
